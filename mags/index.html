Evolutionary stasis — key evidence
KapK-anc (~2 Ma) and KapK-mod (S17 living analogue) differ at 2,579 non-damage sites across 1,689,323 callable positions (99.42% ANI). Comparison with KapK-dec (co-extracted modern fraction) yields 942 non-damage differences. Both genomes exceed 98% ANI with the closest extant relatives. Under an explicit two-clock model the posterior rate ratio is ~244-fold (P(>10×) = 0.948); the strict-clock lower bound is 82-fold suppression relative to background Methanoflorentales. Molecular dating recovers the tip age at 1.92 Ma without a geological prior (95% HPD 1.87–2.11 Ma).
