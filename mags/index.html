Evolutionary stasis — key evidence
Codon alignment of 38 archaeal marker genes (78 taxa, 4,406 nt) shows only 2 SNPs between the ancient (~2 Ma) and modern Kap København Methanoflorentales. Neither substitution is a damage-type (C→T or G→A). The ancient genome is within the species boundary (>95% ANI) with extant permafrost relatives and shares >98.5% ANI with the closest references. Molecular dating (BEAST2 codon tip-dating) estimates the ancient–modern MRCA at 2.01 Ma, with a divergence clock rate of 1.13 × 10⁻⁸ substitutions/site/year.
