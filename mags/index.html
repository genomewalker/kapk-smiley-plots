Evolutionary stasis — key evidence
The KapK ancient consensus (2 Ma) differs from the co-extracted modern fraction by only 942 non-damage SNPs (99.94% ANI), and from the S17 living analogue by 2,579 non-damage SNPs (99.42% ANI). The ancient genome falls within the species boundary (>95% ANI) with all extant Methanoflorentales and shares >98% ANI with the closest references (NAY3300025461b7, SOIL100000032). Molecular dating (BEAST2 codon tip-dating) estimates a clock rate of 1.13 × 10⁻⁸ substitutions/site/year and places the ancient–modern MRCA at ~2.01 Ma.
